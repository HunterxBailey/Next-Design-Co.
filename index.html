FAQ

Good questions.

What happens if it snows during school?

Daytime snow gets cleared starting at 2:30 PM, with season clients first. If you need your driveway clear by a set time, tell me when you sign up and I'll plan the route around it.

What counts as a "storm" on the season plan?

Any snowfall of 2 inches or more. Light dustings under 2" aren't included, but I'll clear them if you ask. Storms over 12" are still covered on the season plan, with a mid-storm pass when it's safe.

The city plow filled in my driveway again. Do you come back?

Season clients get one free return visit per storm to clear the plow bank at the end of the driveway. Per-storm clients can add a return visit for $10.

Do you go up on roofs?

No. For safety, I only rake roofs from the ground with a long-handled roof rake. For steep or high roofs, I'll refer you to a pro.

How do I pay?

Per-storm visits are paid after the work, by cash, Venmo, or check. Season plans are paid up front, or in two payments (half by Nov 15, half by Jan 15).

What if you're sick or away?

I have a trusted backup helper for busy storms and days I can't make it. You'll always get a text letting you know who's coming and when.
