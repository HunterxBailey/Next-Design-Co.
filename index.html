Behind the Lens

I'm Myles Walker, a freelance photographer from Lewiston, Maine. When I'm not on the sideline of a Friday-night game, I'm a junior in high school running MW.P1CS — my freelance studio built for the athletes, students, and events that make our community loud.

I shoot fast, I shoot honest, and I deliver on time. Whether it's varsity basketball at LHS, a school production, or a local tournament, my job is simple: give you photos worth posting, printing, and remembering.
