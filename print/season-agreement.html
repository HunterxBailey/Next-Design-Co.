5Terms, in plain English

  1. Season. Service runs from November 15, 2026 through April 1, 2027.
  2. What counts as a storm. Provider clears the areas checked on page 1 after every snowfall of 2 inches or more. Snowfall under 2 inches isn't included, but Client can ask for a visit at the per-storm rate.
  3. When service happens. Provider is a full-time high school student and works weekdays 5:00–7:15 AM and 2:30–8:00 PM, plus any time on weekends and school snow days. Overnight snow is usually cleared on the morning route, and snow that falls during school is cleared starting at 2:30 PM. Exact arrival times aren't guaranteed. Provider will text before and after each visit.
  4. Big storms. For storms over 12 inches, Provider will make one pass during the storm when it's safe and a final pass after it ends, at no extra charge.
  5. Plow bank. One return visit per storm to clear the pile the city plow leaves at the end of the driveway is included.
  6. Flat price. The season total stays the same no matter how many storms there are. There's no refund for a light winter and no extra charge for a heavy one.
  7. Missed storms. If Provider can't clear a qualifying storm, Client gets a refund or credit of that driveway's per-storm rate ($25 1-car, $35 2-car, or as agreed for large). Provider may send a trusted backup helper and will text Client who is coming.
  8. Client's part. Before the first snow, Client will mark driveway edges, garden beds, and anything low (like drains or edging) with stakes or reflectors. Client will keep pets inside during visits, keep hoses, toys, and decorations out of the way, and tell Provider about hazards.
  9. Ice. Snow can melt and refreeze after Provider leaves. Provider applies ice melt only if checked on page 1. Client is responsible for watching for and treating ice between visits.
  10. Safety. Provider does not climb on roofs; any roof raking is done from the ground only. Provider may delay service during unsafe conditions (freezing rain, lightning, extreme cold, or dangerous roads) and will clear as soon as it's safe.
  11. Care & damage. Provider will work carefully. Provider isn't responsible for damage to items that were buried under snow and not marked, or for damage caused by city plows. Client should tell Provider about any concern within 48 hours so it can be worked out fairly.
  12. Where snow goes. Snow is piled on Client's property, not pushed into the street or onto public sidewalks.
  13. Photos. Provider may take a photo of the cleared area after a visit as a record of service. No photos of people.
  14. Ending early. Either side can end this agreement early by written notice (a text counts). Provider will refund the amount paid minus the per-storm rate for each visit already completed.
  15. Parent or guardian. Provider is under 18. The parent or guardian signing below has read this agreement and co-signs it together with Provider.
